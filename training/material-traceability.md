<section class="section shell"><div class="section-heading"><div><p class="eyebrow"><span></span> Course outcome</p><h2>Turn paperwork into verified material identity.</h2></div><p>The course separates record review, physical identity, traceability transfer, heat-map control, discrepancy handling, and final turnover documentation.</p></div><div class="support-grid"><article class="support-card"><h3>Review material records</h3><p>Identify specifications, grades, heat or lot numbers, product descriptions, test results, certifications, revisions, and missing or conflicting information.</p></article><article class="support-card"><h3>Maintain traceability</h3><p>Preserve identity through receiving, storage, cutting, fit-up, fabrication, transfer marking, heat mapping, and final documentation.</p></article><article class="support-card"><h3>Document discrepancies</h3><p>Recognize gaps, quarantine uncertain material, escalate technical questions, and preserve objective evidence and disposition authority.</p></article></div></section>
