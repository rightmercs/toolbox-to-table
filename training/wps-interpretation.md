  <div class="section-heading"><div><p class="eyebrow"><span></span> Course outcome</p><h2>Move from document reading to controlled review.</h2></div><p>The course connects procedure requirements, qualification support, production conditions, and field documentation without replacing the governing code or the organization’s quality system.</p></div>
  <div class="support-grid">
    <article class="support-card"><h3>Find the controlling basis</h3><p>Identify the governing construction code, adopted edition, project requirements, approved WPS, supporting PQR, and applicable qualification records.</p></article>
    <article class="support-card"><h3>Review systematically</h3><p>Trace variables and qualified ranges through a repeatable workflow that separates observation from compliance determination.</p></article>
    <article class="support-card"><h3>Document and escalate</h3><p>Record objective findings, recognize stop-and-escalate conditions, and preserve technical disposition authority.</p></article>
  </div>
</section>
